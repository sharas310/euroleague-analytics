EuroLeague Impact Lab

Five EuroLeague seasons (2021-22 to 2025-26) pulled from the official API and modelled in Python. Three questions: how much does a team lose when a key player is missing, which defenses shut down which positions, and does knowing the opponent actually make a points forecast better?

How it works

Every test is chronological inside each season: the first 80% of games train the model, the last 20% check it. No information from the future leaks into the forecast, including player roles.

  • CollectBox scores for every game since 2021 through the euroleague-api package, with resumable downloads and back-off on rate limits.euroleague_download.py
  • Rate teamsRidge regression on game margins gives each team a strength rating and a home-court value, so every result can be compared with what was expected.fit_ratings()
  • Assign rolesThe API only says Guard, Forward or Center. K-means on per-40 profiles (assists, rebounds, blocks, 3-point rate) splits players into PG, SG, SF, PF and C.assign_roles()
  • Model and testFour points models, from a per-minute average to a regularised Poisson GLM with opponent×role effects, scored on MAE and RMSE in the held-out 20%.part2_matchups()

Limitations

  • "Without" samples are small for most players; a player who missed two games can show a 20-point swing. Shrinkage helps, but treat single names with caution.
  • The two impact measures barely agree (correlation 0.05 for players with 5+ missed games), another sign of how noisy single-player effects are.
  • Absences are taken from box scores, so rest days and injuries are not separated.
  • Roles come from clustering, not official positions; a hand-made positions file can override them.

Next steps

  • Forecast minutes as their own model (rotation, foul trouble, back-to-backs), since that is where the error is.
  • Use play-by-play lineups to measure individual defenders, already prepared in the code.
  • Combine seasons with decaying weights instead of testing each season alone.
  • Move the pipeline into a scheduled job with a small SQL store.
